01 / The stakes

Every minute is acres — and acres are everything.

The earliest hour of a wildfire is the only hour an incident commander can still choose the outcome. After that, the fire chooses. FireFly is built to compress that hour to minutes.

95%

Of small fires stay small — if you catch them in time.

A fire under 10 acres at first response stays under 10 acres roughly 95% of the time. Every minute past ignition narrows that window.

USFS Initial-Attack Effectiveness Report
1–6hours revisit

Polar-orbit satellites are too slow to see ignition.

By the time MODIS or VIIRS confirms a hotspot, a wind-driven fire has already done its first run. Drones on patrol catch the smoke plume the moment it forms.

NASA FIRMS data product
$893B

Estimated annual U.S. wildfire cost.

Suppression, property loss, healthcare, ecosystem damage — the bill compounds every season. Early detection is the single highest-leverage intervention available.

2024 Joint Economic Committee estimate
73%

Of acres burned in the West come from megafires — fires that started small.

Every megafire was once a tendril of smoke nobody saw. FireFly is designed to see them while they're still tendrils.

02 / The pipeline

Spark to map pin in seconds.

Loop runtime Avg. 4.8s ignition → operator alert
Two-drone cross-bearing fix
01t = 0.0 s

Patrol

Drones broadcast GPS, heading, altitude over HTTPS at 1 Hz. POST /api/drone

02t ≈ 1.8 s

Detect

YOLOv8 scores each MJPEG frame for smoke & flame. Sustained signal >2s triggers an alert.

03t ≈ 3.5 s

Triangulate

Two bearing rays from heading + pixel offset + HFOV intersect to a single GPS coordinate.

04t ≈ 4.8 s

Alert

Pin drops on the operator map with confidence, source drone, and camera preview. GET /api/state
